They Left Behind

Thesis

The United States government’s own primary sources — statutes, treaties, cartographic records, and court rulings — document that American Indians (classified under colonial reclassification as Negroes, Moors, Mullattoes, and Indians) existed on this land before U.S. formation, were reclassified without consent through instruments void under basic contract law, and retain an original political status that was never lawfully extinguished.
